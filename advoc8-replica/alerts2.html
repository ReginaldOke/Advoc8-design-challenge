Choosing your alert frequency

When choosing your alert frequency, consider the feed and how important it is to be notified in real-time versus in a scheduled digest. You can use the preview pane on the right to get a sense of volume and choose a frequency that feels right for you.

  • ASAP alerts are sent as soon as we receive them in Pulse. This should be used for urgent matters.
  • Daily Digests are delivered at 8:00 AM each day with content from the previous 24 hours.
  • Twice Daily Digests are delivered at 8:00 AM and 3:00 PM each day.
  • Weekly Digests are delivered at 8:00 AM Monday each week.
