SA Department of Primary Industries and RegionsSA

Media Release • 8 September

New research to take aim at pinkeye

Dairy AustraliaFederal

Media Release • 9 September

  • Five-year project to investigate causes of infectious bovine keratoconjunctivitis (pinkeye) in dairy cattle.
  • Research aims to identify strains of Moraxella bovis and develop control strategies for farms.
  • Initiative initiated by concerns from farmers, ranking pinkeye as a major health issue.
  • Severe outbreaks can affect up to 80% of calves and require intensive management from farmers.
  • Project will include pilot data collection on farms and genomic analysis of bacteria to inform findings.
