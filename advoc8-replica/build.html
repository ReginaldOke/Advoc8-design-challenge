Keyword Tips

Boolean Query

Boolean Basics

Combine terms with AND, OR, NOT and NEAR

Use parentheses to group terms e.g (cat OR dog) AND NOT mouse

Use quotes to search for an exact phrase e.g "Housing Affordability"

Use * to search for partial words e.g school*

Proximity Searches

By default NEAR will match content where the terms are within 4 words of each other. You can change this by adding a number after NEAR e.g. NEAR/10 will match content where the terms are within 10 words of each other.

e.g school NEAR/2 funding will match "funding of schools", "school funding", "school sports funding" etc.
