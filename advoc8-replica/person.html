Biography

Anthony Albanese is Australia's 31st Prime Minister and has represented the Federal seat of Grayndler in Sydney's inner west since 1996.

Before entering Parliament he worked as a bank officer, political adviser and party official, and he served in senior infrastructure and transport portfolios before becoming Leader of the Australian Labor Party in 2019.

He led Labor to victory at the May 2022 federal election and was re-elected as Prime Minister in 2025. His stated policy focus areas include cost-of-living relief, healthcare, housing and clean-energy manufacturing.
